How the story works & privacy

Each of the 40 choices has an editorial scene and theme stored in the app. Four prewritten story structures combine all ten selections into a short article. Different wording is not a new assessment. No AI service, database, score, or psychological inference is used. Liking the moon does not prove someone is gentle, and liking the sun does not prove someone is harsh.

Answers are held only in page memory. No cookies, IndexedDB, localStorage, external fonts, analytics, or network calls are used by this app. Clear everything removes your current answers and story. Downloads are saved by your browser and must be deleted separately. Your browser or device may keep its own history or screenshots.

Do not use this experience for medical, employment, relationship compatibility, or other consequential decisions. Your own experience matters more than these creative associations.
