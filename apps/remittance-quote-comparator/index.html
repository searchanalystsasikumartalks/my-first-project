Compare up to four money-transfer quotes on the same total sending budget. Enter the rates and fees you have been quoted. No live rates, provider ratings, or financial recommendations.

Fees are paid from the total budget. Percentage fees apply to the amount converted, not the total budget. Receiving fees are deducted in the receiving currency. If a provider uses a different fee basis, convert it to an equivalent fixed fee and set its percentage to zero.

How the numbers work

Amount converted = (total budget − fixed sending fee) ÷ (1 + percentage fee / 100). Amount received = amount converted × exchange rate − receiving fee.

Reference difference = budget × reference rate − amount received. It includes fees and rate differences and can be negative. Actual settlement, taxes, timing, limits and intermediary charges may differ. Confirm all details with the provider.
