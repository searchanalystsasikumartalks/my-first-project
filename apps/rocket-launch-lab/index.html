02 / What happened?

A rocket rises when upward thrust exceeds downward gravity. This simplified lesson holds mass constant and ignores air resistance.

Peak height: —

Height over time: horizontal = elapsed time, vertical = height. Axes scale per launch; compare the numerical peak, not chart shapes.

A model, not a real launch plan

No backend, account, installation or external requests. Results stay in this tab until you download them. Reloading clears the experiment.

Assumptions: vertical motion, constant mass, constant gravity, constant thrust during the powered phase, no drag, no fuel-mass change, no staging or orbit. Ground contact stops the model. These results are not suitable for building or launching a real rocket.

Try: compare 1× and 2× lift; then change only duration; finally compare Earth with the Moon. This is an educational simulation, not a prediction of real flight.
